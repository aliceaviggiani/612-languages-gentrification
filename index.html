Languages spoken at home in the US

The study seeks to visualize and interpret how the process of gentrification in US tracts relates to the languages spoken in households across the four most populous metropolitan areas. It investigates whether Census tracts with high gentrification scores coincide with a high concentration of residents who speak particular languages at home, such as Spanish, Western European, and Slavic languages.
